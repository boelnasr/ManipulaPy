4ManipulaPy Native URDF Parser 

5 

6A modern, optimized URDF parser built specifically for ManipulaPy. 

7Tailored for robotics kinematics and dynamics workflows without a legacy 

8external URDF parser dependency. 

9 

10Features: 

11 - Zero external URDF dependencies (only numpy required for core) 

12 - NumPy 2.0+ compatible 

13 - Direct SerialManipulator/ManipulatorDynamics conversion 

14 - Optional visualization (lazy-loaded trimesh/pybullet) 

15 - Xacro macro expansion support 

16 

17Example: 

18 >>> from ManipulaPy.urdf import URDF 

19 >>> robot = URDF.load("robot.urdf") 

20 >>> manipulator = robot.to_serial_manipulator() 

21 >>> robot.show()
